What changed
  1. Two tabs: Effects (what you use every session) and Settings (set once).
  2. "Disable all" becomes one big Effects on/off switch in the header, so it is always one click away for before/after comparing.
  3. Preset row reads left to right: pick a preset, see if it has unsaved changes, save.
  4. "Starred only / Show hidden" become one selector with counts: All / Starred / Hidden. Active on top stays as a button next to it: one press moves every effect that is on to the top of the list.
  5. Each row keeps move up/down, star, Hide and Open as before; on/off becomes a switch at the start of the row, and the arrows sit together as one group. Buttons keep short names; pointing at one shows what it does.
  6. Pin, bigger/smaller and close are small icon buttons with hover names.
  7. The UEVR depth warning stays as a banner above the list (only shown in UEVR games).
  8. Graphics API, resolution and version stay visible in a quiet line at the bottom.

Settings tab
  1. Everything you set once lives here, in named groups.
  2. Each setting is one line: plain name on the left, its control on the right.
  3. Labels say what a setting does; longer explanations appear when you point at the control, never as extra text you must read.
  4. Restart-needed settings are clearly marked in their own group.
  5. Gestures is its own group: the head gesture switches preset while the menu is closed, so it does not belong with opening the menu. A short text at the top of the group says what gestures do; the row is called "Next preset" and its list reads "Off", "Head Gesture: Left Trigger" and so on, and plain "L3+R3" (not a head gesture). L3+R3 is greyed out with "(already opens this menu)" when the controller buttons above are also L3+R3.
  6. "Always show FPS" leaves the main header and joins the FPS counter group.
